What is SHRUG?

ONE TINY HOBBY
AT A TIME.

Not another productivity app. Not a course platform. SHRUG is a curiosity companion — it helps you figure out who you are through what you make, explore, and notice.

📸 Photography
🍳 Cooking
✍️ Writing
🎨 Painting
🧘 Yoga
+15 more
🎯
Made for curious people
SHRUG is for people who want to explore creative hobbies but aren't sure where to start — or who started everything and finished nothing. The quiz finds your type. The app does the rest.
📖
Learn the real fundamentals
Every hobby comes with interactive concept visuals that teach the actual theory — not just "here's a tutorial." Rule of Thirds. Layered Salting. Show Don't Tell. The ideas that change how you see forever.
✨
Build a memoir as you go
Every reflection, every challenge, every score gets saved to your Memoir — a living record of who you've been and who you're becoming. Most apps track your streaks. SHRUG tracks your growth.
How it works

THREE STEPS.
ONE CHAPTER
AT A TIME.

01
🧠
Take the Quiz
Six questions about how you move through the world. No right answers. SHRUG reads between the lines to match you with your curiosity type and your first three hobbies.
02
🔬
Learn the Concept
Each hobby opens with an interactive concept — the fundamental idea behind the craft. Animations, visuals, and real explanation. Not a video. Not a listicle. The actual thing.
03
🎯
Do the Challenge
Every chapter ends with a real creative challenge. Submit your work. Get AI feedback. Reflect on what surprised you. The answer goes into your Memoir. One chapter becomes one story.

The chapter

NOT A TUTORIAL.
A REAL LESSON.

01
The Concept
The fundamental idea behind the craft — taught visually, not verbally. You'll see it, interact with it, and feel it click.
02
The Challenge
A real creative task built around the concept. Short, specific, and designed to surprise you with what you're actually capable of.
03
The Reflection
One honest question about what just happened. Your answer goes into your Memoir. This is how the story builds.

Curiosity types

WHICH ONE
ARE YOU?

The quiz places you in one of five curiosity types. Each one comes with matched hobbies, a description that's a little too accurate, and a path forward.

🎨
The Creative Soul
You process the world through making things. Ideas come faster than you can execute them.
⚡
The Active Explorer
You learn by doing. Sitting still is your enemy. You want to feel it in your body.
🌿
The Nature Seeker
You recharge outdoors. You notice things others walk past. Slowness is your superpower.
🔬
The Deep Learner
You want to understand how things work at a fundamental level. Mastery over novelty.
☁️
The Calm Creator
You create to decompress. Repetitive, meditative crafts are your happy place.
